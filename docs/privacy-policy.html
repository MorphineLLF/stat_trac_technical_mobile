Stat Trac Technical – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Stat Trac Technical is a work app used by field service technicians of [COMPANY LEGAL NAME] ("we", "us") and its client companies to record the testing, calibration and servicing of medical equipment. This policy explains what personal information the app handles, why, and your rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

The app is for authorised staff only. It cannot be used without an account issued by your employer, and it is not intended for children.

1. Information the app collects

InformationWhoseWhy
Username and passwordTechnician To sign in. The password is sent to our server over an encrypted connection to verify it and is not stored on the device.
Name and user IDTechnician To record who carried out and issued each certificate.
SignatureTechnician To sign test certificates as evidence of the work done.
Name and signatureFacility contact (the client's representative who signs a certificate) To record who accepted the work on the client's behalf, where the certificate requires it.
Test results, notes, dates and equipment detailsBusiness records The content of the test certificate itself.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, photos, files, advertising identifiers, browsing activity or usage analytics, and it contains no advertising.

2. Device permissions

  • Camera – only to scan equipment barcodes. Images are read on the device to find the barcode and are not saved or sent anywhere.
  • Internet and network state – to send and receive certificates, and to wait for a connection when there is none.

3. Where the information is kept

The app works offline. Certificates and the equipment and template lists a technician needs are stored on the device until they can be sent. When a connection is available they are sent to our server over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Sign-in tokens are kept in Android's secure storage.

The server and its databases are hosted in [COUNTRY / DATA CENTRE – e.g. South Africa]. Each client company's records are kept in a separate database.

4. Who can see it

Certificates are available to the client company they belong to and to our authorised staff who administer the service. We do not sell personal information and do not share it with third parties for marketing. We disclose it only where the law requires us to.

5. How long it is kept

Test certificates are regulatory and safety records and are kept for [RETENTION PERIOD, e.g. as long as the equipment is in service plus N years], or longer where the law requires. Information on a device is removed when the app is uninstalled.

6. Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to its processing. Because certificates are records of work performed, a signature on an issued certificate may need to be kept even after a request to delete it; if so, we will tell you why.

You may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa: inforegulator.org.za.
