- **No Next on a machine with an open work order.**

  Add part opens a search over the synced register (parts only), or "Type it
  instead"; then a quantity (decimals, a comma counts). Each line has a remove
  button, and there are no prices.
  - The parts go up in `capture.parts`. A job with parts waits until the
    server lists `capture_parts`.
  - The detail screen lists the parts, from the queue or from synced
    `RepairPart`.

  - Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-02-work-order-parts.md`.

  in the app bar of a synced work order (`WorkOrderDocumentActions`). On a
  queued job they are disabled, with "Sync first".
  - The PDF is always fetched fresh, because a work order can change after it
    is printed. The saved copy opens only when there is no signal, and then
    says so.
  - Go side: `workOrderDocPath` and `wo_email_json.go` (commit f08f2ca),
    deployed to `demo`.
  - The email box and the document results are shared in

  - Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-02-work-order-email-design.md`.
